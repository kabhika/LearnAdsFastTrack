# Ads Fast-Track Line

The sprint companion to the Ads Mastery Line: 40 stations, ~45 minutes
each, six a day by default — from never-run-an-ad to your first client
campaign live, tracked and reported, in one working week. Same engine as
the LLM Mastery, Marketing Mastery, Business AI Conduit and Ads Mastery
lines.

- **Now boarding** — the next station, with links.
- **Network map** — the whole sprint as a snaking metro line; the pulsing
  ring is where you are.
- **Departures** — the self-adjusting schedule. Skip a day and every
  remaining station shifts forward automatically.
- **All stations** — every station with a checkbox and a private log
  entry.

Built for a working developer who already ships client sites: tracking
stations assume Next.js on Vercel, the pitch station is the client
conversation, and the final phase turns the skill into an offer. Free
first; the only paid station (the optional Meta certification exam) is
flagged. Progress lives in this browser via localStorage
(`ads_fasttrack_v1`); export a JSON backup from **Service**. Sprint pace
adjustable there too (2/4/6/8 stations a day).

Day 0 the money model · Day 1 Google campaign · Day 2 the tracking gate ·
Days 3–4 launch & optimize · Days 5–6 Meta · Day 7+ the service wrap.
